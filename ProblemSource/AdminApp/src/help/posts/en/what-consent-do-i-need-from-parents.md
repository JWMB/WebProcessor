---
title: What consent do I need from the parents?
categories: faq
posted: 'Sep 26, 2026'
---

Every parent receives an information letter about the study before the training starts. What they have to do depends on which study their class is taking part in.

#### The main study — parents only act if they object

Parents do not have to sign anything for their child to take part. The letter explains the study and asks them to get in touch only if they do **not** want their child to participate. If a parent says no, that child simply does not train with Vektor and is not entered in the group.

#### The maths anxiety sub-study — written consent required

Some classes also take part in a second study about maths anxiety. That one needs a written yes or no from the parents. The consent slip is at the bottom of the information letter; there is no separate form. **All parents hand the slip back**, including those who agree to everything, because the slip is where they tick yes or no.

Keep the returned slips at school until we visit — we collect them then. Do not send them by post or email.

#### The Consent box in the teacher admin page

In Step 3, when you fill in Gender and Born for each pupil, tick the box in the **Consent** column for every pupil who is taking part. Leave it unticked for any pupil whose parent has said no — that pupil will not be able to train.

Ticking the box means: the parents have been informed and have not objected. For classes in the maths anxiety sub-study it also means the slip has come back with a yes.

If you are unsure about a particular pupil, leave the box unticked and email vektorproject2026@gmail.com before the training starts.
