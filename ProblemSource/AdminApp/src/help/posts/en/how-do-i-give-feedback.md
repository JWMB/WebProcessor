---
title: How do I give feedback on the training?
categories: faq
posted: 'Sep 26, 2026'
---

Email **vektorproject2026@gmail.com**. There is no feedback form in the app — we would rather hear it in your own words.

We are particularly interested in:

- exercises the pupils find confusing and why
- words, pictures or sounds that seem wrong or hard to make out
- anything in the teacher admin page that is more work than it needs to be
- how the 20 minutes a day fits into your school day in practice

Vektor 2.0 is being developed as the study runs, and teachers' reports are how most problems have been found so far. Small observations are welcome — you do not need to write a report.
