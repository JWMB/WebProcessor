---
title: A pupil gets an error about consent when starting
categories: faq
posted: 'Sep 26, 2026'
---

A pupil cannot start training until three things are filled in for them in the teacher admin page: **Gender**, **Born** and **Consent**. If any one of them is missing, the pupil gets an error instead of the training.

To fix it:

1. Open the group in the teacher admin page.
2. Find the pupil in the list.
3. Check that Gender is chosen, that Born has both a year and a date, and that the box in the Consent column is ticked or not (depending on the parent's decision).
4. Let the pupil try again.

This is the most common cause. It usually happens to a pupil who was added to the group after the rest of the class, since it is easy to fill in the first list and forget the late addition.

If all three are filled in and the error is still there, email vektorproject2026@gmail.com with PROBLEM in the subject line, and tell us the group name and the pupil's anonymised name.
