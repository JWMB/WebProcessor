---
title: How do I follow my class's training?
categories: faq
posted: 'Sep 26, 2026'
---

Everything is on the teacher admin page. Log in and open your group, and you see the whole class in one list.

To see one pupil in detail, click the arrow (^) next to their anonymised name. That opens their progress week by week.

#### What to look for

- **Whether a pupil is training at all.** A pupil who has not started, or who stopped after a few days, is the one thing worth acting on. The rest usually sorts itself out.
- **Days missed.** A pupil who is away does not fall behind — they carry on where they left off next time. No need to catch up.

#### What you do not need to do

You do not need to check the page daily, and you do not need to do anything with what you see. The app adjusts the difficulty for each pupil by itself, so a pupil moving more slowly than the rest is not a sign that something is wrong — they are simply on the level that suits them. The pupils do not see each other's progress.

In week 7 the closing maths test runs automatically in the app.

If a pupil's progress looks stuck, or the list shows nothing at all for someone who has been training, email vektorproject2026@gmail.com with PROBLEM in the subject line.
