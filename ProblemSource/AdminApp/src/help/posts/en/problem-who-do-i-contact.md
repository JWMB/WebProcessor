title: PROBLEM — who do I contact?
categories: faq

---

Email **vektorproject2026@gmail.com** and put **PROBLEM** in the subject line. That gets it in front of us quickly.

Write to us about anything that stops the training: pupils who cannot log in, an error message, a tablet that will not load the app, a pupil who has to stop taking part, or anything in the teacher admin page that does not behave as the guide describes.

To help us answer in one reply rather than three, include:

- your name and school
- the name of the group
- the anonymised pupil name, if it concerns one pupil
- what happens, and what you expected to happen
- a screenshot if you have one

Do not include pupils' real names, personal identity numbers or other personal data in the email.
