We are pleased to announce that Vektor is being resurrected this spring with the help of research funding from a Wallenberg Foundation and based in the Karolinska Institutet.

The interface looks and works just like before - however you access it via login on a website, not through an app installed through Apples App Store or Google Play.

For any extra information, please visit the Vektor 2.0 website : https://ellagruber.github.io/Vektor_website/
